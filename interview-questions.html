How to run it

Four notes before you hit record.

01

Conversation, not a form

Read the bold question aloud and let the answer breathe. The italic line under each is an optional probe — only use it if they stall or you want more.

02

Record clean audio

Phone voice memo or a quiet room is enough. Transcription needs clear speech, so one voice at a time — restate the question if they talk over it.

03

Follow the tags

Each question shows which page it feeds and its status chip. Need items are the gaps that block the build — prioritize those.

04

Skip what doesn't fit

Not every block applies to every sitting. Do Bob first; the partner, student, and counselor blocks are separate, repeatable interviews.

  • Walk me through the three sectors — Advanced Manufacturing, Health Sciences, Building Trades — and what each one actually covers.

  • I'll read back the 7 draft FAQ answers we already have on the homepage — tell me where any are wrong or need softening. (Watch the undocumented-eligibility one especially.)
    Reality check: those 7 (in home.html) are the only drafted Q&As — their answers are unverified, so this is the accuracy pass on them. The fuller “20–30” master list the outline calls for still has to be written; the next question starts gathering it.
    The 7 drafted answers · read each aloud, get Bob's yes / needs-work
    Do I need a 4.0?
    No. We don't care about your class rank. We care whether you show up, whether you can commit to the training, and whether the sector you're picking actually excites you. A 2.5 with a clear plan beats a 4.0 with no direction. Every time.
    Is there an essay?
    Technically there's one short-answer prompt at the end (“tell us in a few words why you want to do this”). No word count. No trick questions. No “describe a challenge you overcame.” Just be yourself. Three good sentences beat three good paragraphs.
    What if I'm not sure which sector yet?
    Pick the one that feels closest. We'll pair you with someone working in all three so you can ask the real questions — hours, wages, what they wish they knew at 18. You can switch tracks up until your training actually starts.
    What does it actually cost me?
    Zero dollars. That's not marketing — that's the mechanics. JCD pays tuition direct to your program, sends a gear stipend to you, and connects you to employer partners. No fees. No hidden application cost. No catch.
    What if I want to go to a 4-year school later?
    Totally fine. Most of our paths include transferable college credit. A lot of scholars end up going back for a bachelor's in their late 20s — paid for by an employer. Starting in the trades isn't closing a door. It's opening a different one.
    I'm undocumented. Can I still apply?Verify carefully
    Yes. JCD scholarships are funded privately by the Dunham family — not federally — so immigration status doesn't affect eligibility. If you graduated from one of our qualifying schools and want to train into one of our three sectors, you're in the running. (Highest-stakes claim on the page — confirm this is actually true before launch.)
    How long does the application take?
    Eight minutes if you move fast. [TK] Five questions, a short answer at the end, done. Save and come back if you need to. We'll confirm we got it within 48 hours.
