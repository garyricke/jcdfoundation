JCD / Scholarship application / 2027

The JCD Foundation Scholarship application.

For students from our qualifying Fox Valley high schools heading into community college, a trade or vocational program, or a university. There are no essays to polish and no GPA box. We want to know where you're going and what it will cost to get there.

Have these ready before you start (or start now and add them later — everything saves on this device):

  • Your career goal, in under 300 words
  • A cost estimate from the school or program you'll attend
  • That school's financial aid office contact
  • Two letters of recommendation, from a teacher and/or a boss
  • A request to your high school to email us your official transcript
