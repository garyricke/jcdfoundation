   "It's gonna be case by case" · "We don't want to put a top range. I don't want to say 50% because some kids may need 100%." The Dunham Foundation story: a published $1M ceiling meant "Everybody in the world asks for a million bucks."

   **How he'll judge need without financial info:** by where the student lives and goes to school ("if the kid lives over on … East Side … he has greater needs than the kid that is applying from Geneva").

4. **Eligibility edge cases → gap-year, part-time and already-enrolled students are all fine. Undocumented: we don't address it.**

6. **Board → no board list on the site.**

### C. Outreach plan (Bob + Jack)

- Also **COD, Waubonsee, Aurora University**, since currently enrolled students qualify.

### D. Application admin + review tool (big one)
Bob wants Gary "intimately involved in the administration … because I'm not tech." Gary proposed building it, and Bob agreed:
- **Query/filter** by ZIP ("give me all 60510"), high school, focus area. "I want to be able to query."
- **Reviewer actions:** notes, and status buttons for *reviewed / tier 1 / tier 2 / rejected*. Filter by status ("let me see all the tier ones … what haven't been reviewed yet").
- **Distance tiers:** students at the edge of the service area (e.g. far side of Oswego) land in a **second tier**.
- **AI flagging.** It suggests which applications to look at first and **does not decide**. "I like that. A lot." That could also let the 300-word cap loosen, since AI handles the reading load.
- **What reviewers look for** (Bob): not grades and not the ACT ("If a kid's got a C average, I don't care"). He wants their story and what they want to do, and whether they're active and held a job: "If you held a job and you went to school, God bless you. If you'd zippo … you're a risk."
- The first cycle is a **pilot**: "We'll make modifications."
- **Gary owns:** a proposal for ongoing support and admin (this is the Phase 3 retainer conversation).

### E. Welcome video for the site
- Gary: "We don't have any video that somebody could watch … the humanness behind all of this."
- Agreed: **Gary test-pilots** a short welcome video ("AI will write it"), then they decide who fronts it (Gary or someone better). "Let's do that."
- Tone guard from Bob: welcoming and story-first, but **don't** say "we don't care about your grades." "We don't want to send a message to kids: don't worry about doing homework."
- Mission line to anchor it: "We are promoting career paths with financial assistance. For young people, period."
- **Bob's daughter** (Jack's mom, a 42-year-old educator, on the Dunham Foundation board) will be engaged but **behind the scenes**, to keep a degree of separation between the foundations.
