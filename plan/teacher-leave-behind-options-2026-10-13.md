## The brief

- **Audience:** CTE instructors (welding, nursing/CNA, construction, auto, etc.), not counselors. Bob and Jack hand it over during school visits.
- **Quantity:** about a box of 50 per school × 13 high schools ≈ **650**, plus Fox Valley Career Center and the three colleges (COD, Waubonsee, Aurora University) if they get boxes too ≈ **800–850** [TK — confirm sites].
- **Needed:** about late October, so it's in hand around the Nov 1 site launch. Today is Oct 13, which leaves about **2½ weeks**.
- **Every item carries:** JCD logo · one short line · `thejcdfoundation.org/apply` (clean URL, never `.html`) · QR to the same URL where there's room. Optional: "Applications open Dec 1, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027."
- **Suggested line:** "Know a student who wants to build, fix or care? Send them our way." Alternative: "Supporting Career Paths."
- **One version, not three.** Sector flyers are already split by area. The desk item goes to all instructors, so one generic version keeps setup costs and lead time down.

> **Don't reuse `print/leave-behind.html` as-is.** It predates the Oct 6 decisions. ## Recommendation

**Two layers:**

1. **Order sticky-note pads (option 1) now** as the "always in front of them" item Bob described: one color, 3×3, ~25–50 sheets, URL + small QR on every sheet. Get two quotes this week. If a vendor can't promise delivery by **Oct 30**, don't rush-pay. Hand them out on the second round of visits.
2. **Print QR desk tent cards (option 3) at the UPS Store** alongside the sector flyers, so Bob and Jack have something to leave on Nov 1 no matter what. The tent card does the explaining and the sticky notes keep the name in view.

Pens are the low-cost fallback if Coach T says sticky notes don't get used. Skip lanyards.

Packaging: drop a box (or a sleeve of 50 pads/cards) in each school with a short printed note for the CTE department chair: "For your instructors. Send students to thejcdfoundation.org/apply." [TK — wording after Coach T]

**Before ordering:** confirm `thejcdfoundation.org/apply` loads on the live domain (password gate off Nov 1), and test-scan the QR from print proofs.

## Questions for Coach T

1. What's actually on a CTE teacher's desk that they use every day: sticky notes, pens, something else?
2. If you got a box of 50 of something from an outside group, would it reach the other instructors? Who's the right person to hand it to (department chair, front office, staff mailbox)?
3. Do students ever see or scan things on a teacher's desk, or would a teacher only point a student to it?
4. Is there anything teachers find annoying or just throw out (pens, magnets, flyers)?
5. Do districts have any rules about branded items or materials from outside organizations in classrooms?
6. When in the year would teachers most likely talk with seniors about next steps? Is early December (when applications open) a good time to land on their desks?
