How this got made, because it’s worth knowing.

I fed the raw footage in and AI did the rest. It transcribed all six people, indexed what each of them said, decided which moments were worth using, cut them, cleaned up the audio, pulled the filler words out, wrote the page, and built every association between a clip and the copy sitting next to it. The stat tiles, which quote belongs under which question, the whole argument the page makes — that came out of the interviews themselves, not from me deciding in advance what I wanted them to say.

It also caught two things I would have missed. Two of our people contradict each other on how long the degree takes, so the page handles that carefully instead of picking the more flattering answer. And it flagged that I’d had the two hygienists’ names attached to the wrong quotes.
