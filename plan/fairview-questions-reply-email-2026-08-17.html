The full list

Yours are marked BOB. Read the question and let the answer breathe — we'll cut later.

Set A — the owners, together

Audio, about 20–25 minutes. Gary leads, you add color.

  1. Tell me about Fairview — how big the practice is, and how many dental hygienists do you employ? BOB
  2. How do hygienists contribute to the success of your practice? BOB
  3. What does the current job market look like for recent graduates entering the field — and how hard is it for you to fill a seat? BOB
  4. What is the typical compensation package for a starting hygienist — and where does that go with a few years' experience? BOB
  5. What attributes do you look for when you're searching for hygienists? When you hire someone young, does class rank matter — or something else? BOB
  6. What's the training path into your practice — which programs or credentials, and how long do they take?
  7. If JCD helped fund a local student into that path, would you hire or host them? What would a partnership look like?
  8. Why does supporting local training matter to you as an owner in this community?
  9. May we use Fairview's name and a few photos on the JCD site?

Set B — each hygienist, one at a time

About 8–10 minutes each. Jack co-leads — closer to their age, so it stays natural.

  1. What motivated you to pursue a career in health care — and what drew you to dental hygiene specifically? BOB
  2. Was this an easy decision, or did you feel pressure to do a four-year degree first?
  3. Where did you go to school — how long did the program take, and roughly what did it cost? BOB
  4. Did you have to be a straight-A student to get through it?
  5. What's a typical day like working at a large dental office — and what part of it gives you the most satisfaction? BOB
  6. What surprised you most about the career once you started? What do you wish you'd known at 18? BOB
  7. Without naming a number if you'd rather not — has this been a career you can build a life on?
  8. What would you say to a high-school senior who's unsure whether a four-year college is right for them?
  9. May we share your name, photo, and story on the site?

The last question in each set is the permission ask — worth getting on the recording while the mics are still on.
