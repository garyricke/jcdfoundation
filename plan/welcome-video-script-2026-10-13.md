# JCD welcome video: test-pilot script

**Date:** October 13, 2026 · **Draft by:** Gary Ricke (Orbis Design) · **Status:** test pilot for Bob's review (Oct 6 action item 4E)
**Where it runs:** the website (home page, and possibly `/apply`) · **Length target:** 60–90 seconds

## Brief

- **Job:** give a visitor "the humanness behind all of this" without waiting for an in-person presentation. A person welcomes you and tells you what JCD is, who it's for, and when to apply.
- **Anchor line** (Oct 6, the IRS charter language): "We are promoting career paths with financial assistance. For young people, period."
- **Tone:** warm, plain, story-first. Talks to a 17-year-old, with a parent or teacher who might be watching too.
- **Tone guards:**
  - Never say or imply grades don't matter or homework is optional. ("We don't want to send a message to kids: don't worry about doing homework.") The script asks for their story and effort, and says nothing about grades either way.
  - **Pre-launch.** Nobody has been funded yet. No "students we've helped", no impact numbers, no recipient stories.
  - No dollar amounts, caps or percentages. Say "tuition and selected expenses" only.
  - The founder isn't named or shown. The funding source isn't named. John C. Dunham, the namesake, gets one light mention.
- **Who fronts it:** written in the foundation's "we" voice with no presenter name in the script, so **Gary can test-pilot it and someone else can replace him** without rewrites. If a lower third is used, it reads just "JCD Foundation" (or the presenter's first name + "JCD Foundation") [TK — decide after pilot]. Not the founder, per the "not the face" rule.

### Line notes

- **Line 4** is Bob's sentence word for word. Keep "period."
- **Line 5:** "relied on the trades every day" and "his wife was a nurse" come from the Our Story line Bob confirmed on Oct 6. Don't add company names, cities or dates without checking them against the documentary notes.
- **Line 7** avoids saying who gets paid. Bob is still checking with his accountant about payments to students vs. institutions/employers.
- **Line 8:** "already in a program" covers students currently enrolled at COD, Waubonsee and Aurora University. Confirm the exact eligibility wording against the live `/apply` copy before recording [TK].
- **Line 10** replaces any "we don't care about grades" idea. It encourages effort without ranking students by GPA.
- **Line 11:** keep "people who work in these fields" general. Don't name reviewers.
- **URL:** say and show `thejcdfoundation.org/apply`. Never a `.html` link. Check that it resolves on the live domain before publishing.

## Production notes for the test pilot

- **Shoot:** one presenter setup (Gary for the pilot), lav mic, 2–3 takes per line. Record lines separately so the cutdown can reuse them.
- **B-roll:** pull from existing licensed/owned footage first (Fairview, the hero carousel, archival photos). Any new footage of students needs signed releases, and a parent signature for minors [TK].
- **Captions:** burned in or as a VTT track. Most people will watch muted.
- **Delivery:** host on Cloudinary under `jcd/` using the standard video recipe (webm + mp4, `q_auto`, poster frame). No autoplay with sound.
- **Review loop:** pilot cut goes to Bob. He decides whether Gary or "a better person" fronts the final. Swap the presenter and re-shoot lines 1, 4, 6, 9, 10 and 12. B-roll and graphics carry over.
