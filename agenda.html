The seven decisions
From the Sept 16 email. Bob sent the application spec instead of answering these, so they’re all still open. Each one holds up copy or how the program runs before January 1.
Decide · 10 min
Bob’s calls
  1. What we pay for. Tuition only, or also boots, tools and getting there? What We Fund still promises the extras, but Bob’s script edits took them out.
  2. Who gets the check. The student, the school or the employer. Plus one call to counsel on whether paying students directly needs IRS-approved grant procedures (§4945(g)).
  3. Award size. A published range, or “sized case by case” said plainly.
  4. Eligibility edge cases. Gap-year grads, part-time students, students already enrolled, students reapplying, undocumented students.
  5. Reviews. Who reads the applications, and when and how decisions go out.
  6. The board. The list and bios (first asked July 28).
  7. The nurse line on Our Story. Keep it or replace it. Bob’s own documentary doesn’t support it.
Leave with

Answers to 1, 2, 3 and 5 today. Those change what the site says and how the window runs. Bob can send 4, 6 and 7 by email.
