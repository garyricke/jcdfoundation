Why we're doing it

What this feeds on the site.

Fairview is a Health Sciences employer — one of JCD's three sectors — and the young hygienists walked the exact path JCD is built to fund: a short credential into a stable, well-paid local career. Because the program hasn't launched and there are no funded students yet, this is the honest stand-in for “impact”: we can't show JCD outcomes, but we can show the path itself is real and good.

The questions

Two sets, one visit.

Read the bold question aloud and let the answer breathe. The italic line is an optional probe — use it only if they stall or you want more. Set A

The owner / dentist count [TK]

Interviewed together if there are two · audio · ~20–25 min · Gary leads

The employer voice — the demand side of the career path. We want them making the case that dental hygiene is a real, hard-to-fill, well-paid career, and that grades matter less than showing up. Let them brag about the practice a little.

Hiring, the career path & partnership
/employers/what-we-fund/impact
  • Tell me about Fairview — how big the practice is, and how many dental hygienists do you employ?Bob
    Probe: how many people total, how many locations, how long you've been in Kane County.
  • How do hygienists contribute to the success of your practice?Bob
    Probe: what would a week here look like without them?
  • What does the current job market look like for recent graduates entering the field — and how hard is it for you to fill a seat?Bob
    Probe: any number you'd put on it — open roles, months to fill a seat?
  • What is the typical compensation package for a starting hygienist — and where does that go with a few years' experience?Bob
    What we're listening for

    This is the whole point of the visit — an employer saying out loud that this path pays and lasts. It's stronger coming from an owner than from us, and it's kinder than asking a 24-year-old what they earn.

    • A realistic starting wage and where it goes with a few years' experience.
    • What's in the package beyond the hourly — benefits, PTO, CE, licensing.
    • Schedule reality — full-time, part-time, four-day weeks, flexibility.
    • Growth — lead hygienist, specialties, cross-training, ownership tracks.
  • What attributes do you look for when you're searching for hygienists? When you hire someone young, does class rank matter — or something else?Bob
    Ties straight to our “we don't care about your class rank” message. Probe: what does a great young hire have that a mediocre one doesn't?
  • What's the training path into your practice — which programs or credentials, and how long do they take?
    Probe: which local schools do your good people come from?
  • If JCD helped fund a local student into that path, would you hire or host them? What would a partnership look like?
    Probe: shadow days, guaranteed interviews, co-funding, mentoring?
  • Why does supporting local training matter to you as an owner in this community?
    The heart line for the video — let it be personal.
  • May we use Fairview's name and a few photos on the JCD site?
    Ask it while the recorder is still running so the yes is on tape.
Set B

The young hygienists count [TK]

One at a time · audio + a little video · ~8–10 min each · Jack co-leads

The career-path story — real people who took the exact route JCD funds. Keep it short and human; short clips beat long answers. Ask permission on tape before the mics come off — a recorded yes is what we're going on.

How they got here & what they'd tell an 18-year-old
/what-we-fund/impact
  • What motivated you to pursue a career in health care — and what drew you to dental hygiene specifically?Bob
    Warm-up. Probe: was there a moment you knew this was it?
  • Was this an easy decision, or did you feel pressure to do a four-year degree first?
    The core tension the site speaks to — the one moment a student watching sees themselves instead of a stranger. Probe: what did people around you assume you'd do?
  • Where did you go to school — how long did the program take, and roughly what did it cost?Bob
    What we're listening for

    The single most decision-changing sentence in the whole video — and the number that justifies the grant size on /what-we-fund. Let them round; we don't need it to the dollar.

    • The school / program name and how many months or years it took.
    • A rough cost figure — exactly the gap JCD closes.
    • What the day-to-day of training looked like (clinical hours, boards, licensing).
  • Did you have to be a straight-A student to get through it?
    Bob's “what skills are important for success” re-aimed at the student watching. Probe: what actually got you through — and what didn't matter as much as you expected?
  • What's a typical day like working at a large dental office — and what part of it gives you the most satisfaction?Bob
    Probe: the patient or the moment you think about on the drive home.
  • What surprised you most about the career once you started? What do you wish you'd known at 18?Bob
    Probe: anything you almost let stop you?
  • Without naming a number if you'd rather not — has this been a career you can build a life on?
    Let them set the comfort level. The point is stability, not a salary figure.
  • What would you say to a high-school senior who's unsure whether a four-year college is right for them?
    The video line. Straight to camera if they're up for it.
  • May we share your name, photo, and story on the site?
    Ask it with the recorder still running — that yes on tape is our permission.
