The short version

Three things they told us.

Each one in their own words, from the film.

Hiring

Practices can’t find enough hygienists.

It’s a well-known fact that there’s a shortage of hygienists out there… it’s a great time to get into it. Dr. David Gorenz
$45/hr

Starting pay for a new hygiene graduate at Fairview.

Our office, we’d start them out at $45 an hour. So not a bad gig right out of school. Dr. Salvador Cardenas · Dr. Dominguez and Dr. Gorenz gave the same figure
3–4 yrs

From high school to working, prerequisites included.

About a total of four years… two years of gen eds, prereqs, and then two years in the actual program. Bethany, hygienist · College of DuPage
Where JCD comes in

Dental hygiene is a
health sciences path.

Hygiene programs at community colleges qualify. JCD can help pay tuition, room and board, books and related supplies, up to half of those costs, paid directly to your school.
